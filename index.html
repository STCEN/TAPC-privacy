TAPC 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 9 月 18 日

TAPC（以下简称“本应用”）重视您的隐私。 本隐私政策说明本应用在您使用相关功能时如何收集、使用、 存储和处理您的信息。

1. 通过 Apple 登录

本应用使用“通过 Apple 登录”（Sign in with Apple）提供账户登录功能。 当您选择使用 Apple 登录时，根据您向 Apple 授予的权限， 本应用可能获得以下信息：

  • Apple 提供的用户标识符；
  • 您的姓名；
  • 您的电子邮件地址，包括您选择使用“隐藏邮件地址”时由 Apple 提供的私密转送地址。

这些信息用于识别您的账户、维持登录状态以及提供账户资料同步功能。 本应用不会将这些信息用于第三方广告或跨应用追踪。

2. iCloud 与 CloudKit

为提供账户资料同步功能，本应用使用 Apple 提供的 CloudKit 服务。 与账户有关的部分资料会存储于用户的私有 CloudKit 数据库中。

目前可能存储的账户资料包括：

  • Apple 用户标识符；
  • Apple 登录提供的姓名；
  • Apple 登录提供的电子邮件地址；
  • CloudKit 用户记录标识符；
  • 用户主动选择的个人头像；
  • 账户资料的创建和更新时间。

上述信息仅用于账户识别、账户资料显示以及账户资料同步。

3. 用户头像

当您主动从设备照片库中选择并设置个人头像时， 本应用会将经过您选择和裁剪后的头像保存至 CloudKit， 以用于您的账户资料显示和同步。

本应用不会因为设置头像而上传您的整个照片库。

4. 网球视频与运动分析数据

用户在本应用中录制或导入的网球视频，以及由这些视频产生的运动分析数据， 当前均在用户设备本地进行处理和保存。

当前版本不会通过账户同步功能将以下内容上传至 CloudKit：

  • 用户录制或导入的训练视频；
  • 人体姿态或骨骼分析数据；
  • 挥拍识别结果；
  • 技术评分及分析报告；
  • 训练日历和成长分析数据；
  • 本地应用设置及分析选项。

除本隐私政策另有说明外，上述训练及运动分析数据保留在用户设备本地， 不会上传至开发者服务器。

5. 健康与位置信息

当前账户及 CloudKit 同步功能不会将 Apple Health 健康数据 或用户位置信息上传至 CloudKit。

6. 信息的使用

本应用收集或处理的账户信息仅用于：

  • 验证和识别用户账户；
  • 提供 Apple 登录功能；
  • 同步账户资料；
  • 显示和同步用户头像；
  • 维护应用功能和账户服务。

本应用不会出售您的个人信息，也不会将您的个人信息用于第三方广告 或跨应用、跨网站追踪。

7. 数据共享

本应用依赖 Apple 提供的 Sign in with Apple、iCloud 和 CloudKit 服务完成身份验证及账户资料同步。

除提供这些功能所必需的情况、法律要求或保护用户和服务安全所必需的情况外， 我们不会主动向第三方出售或共享您的个人信息。

8. 数据保留与账户删除

账户资料可能会保存在 CloudKit 中，以便提供账户识别和资料同步功能。

退出登录仅会退出当前设备上的账户登录状态， 并不等同于删除已经存储在 CloudKit 中的账户资料。

如果您希望删除您的账户及与账户相关的云端资料， 您可以通过本应用提供的账户删除功能发起删除， 或通过本隐私政策底部提供的联系方式联系我们。

9. 数据安全

本应用使用 Apple 提供的系统服务和 CloudKit 基础设施处理和存储 与账户相关的数据。我们会采取合理措施保护用户信息， 防止未经授权的访问、使用或披露。

10. 儿童隐私

本应用不会有意收集法律规定需要获得父母或监护人同意的儿童个人信息。 如果我们发现相关个人信息是在未经必要授权的情况下提供的， 将采取适当措施进行处理。

11. 隐私政策的变更

随着应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们可能更新本隐私政策。 更新后的隐私政策将在本页面发布，并更新页面顶部的“最后更新日期”。

TAPC Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

TAPC ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and processed when you use the App.

1. Sign in with Apple

The App uses Sign in with Apple to provide account authentication. Depending on the permissions you grant through Apple, the App may receive:

  • A user identifier provided by Apple;
  • Your name;
  • Your email address, including an Apple private relay email address if you choose the Hide My Email option.

This information is used to identify your account, maintain your sign-in status, and provide account profile synchronization. It is not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. iCloud and CloudKit

The App uses Apple's CloudKit service to provide account profile synchronization. Certain account information may be stored in the user's private CloudKit database.

The account information currently stored may include:

  • Apple user identifier;
  • Name provided through Sign in with Apple;
  • Email address provided through Sign in with Apple;
  • CloudKit user record identifier;
  • A profile image voluntarily selected by the user;
  • Profile creation and update timestamps.

This information is used only for account identification, profile display, and account profile synchronization.

3. Profile Image

If you voluntarily select and set a profile image from your device's photo library, the selected and cropped image may be stored in CloudKit for profile display and synchronization.

The App does not upload your entire photo library when you select a profile image.

4. Tennis Videos and Motion Analysis Data

Tennis videos recorded or imported by users, together with motion analysis data generated from those videos, are currently processed and stored locally on the user's device.

The current version does not upload the following data to CloudKit through the account synchronization feature:

  • Recorded or imported training videos;
  • Human pose or skeletal analysis data;
  • Swing detection results;
  • Technical scores and analysis reports;
  • Training calendar and progress analysis data;
  • Local application settings and analysis preferences.

Unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy, this training and motion analysis data remains locally on the user's device and is not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

5. Health and Location Information

The current account and CloudKit synchronization functionality does not upload Apple Health data or user location information to CloudKit.

6. How We Use Information

Account information is used only to:

  • Authenticate and identify user accounts;
  • Provide Sign in with Apple;
  • Synchronize account profile information;
  • Display and synchronize profile images;
  • Maintain application functionality and account services.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use your personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

7. Data Sharing

The App relies on Apple's Sign in with Apple, iCloud, and CloudKit services for authentication and account profile synchronization.

Except where necessary to provide these services, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and the security of the service, we do not sell or intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account profile information may remain stored in CloudKit in order to provide account identification and profile synchronization.

Signing out only ends the account session on the current device and does not by itself delete account information already stored in CloudKit.

If you wish to delete your account and associated cloud account data, you may initiate account deletion using the account deletion feature provided in the App or contact us using the information below.

9. Data Security

The App uses Apple system services and CloudKit infrastructure to process and store account-related information. Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental or guardian consent is required by applicable law. If we become aware that such information has been provided without the required authorization, appropriate steps will be taken.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App's features or data processing practices change. Updates will be published on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.
